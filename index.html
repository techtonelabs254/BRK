How we work

Four stages, with your approval at each one. You always know what is done and what comes next.

  1. Discuss

    We listen to your goals, your users and your budget, then agree on what will be built.

    You get: a clear plan and quote

  2. Design

    We sketch the layout and choose colors and fonts with you before any code is written.

    You get: a preview to approve

  3. Build

    We code it, connect the data and secure every form and login, testing on phones and computers.

    You get: a working version to try

  4. Launch and support

    We publish it, show you how to use it and fix issues quickly after launch.

    You get: a live product and ongoing help
